How Do Circuits Work?

Electricity plays an important role in our everyday lives. From lighting up our homes to powering electronic gadgets, electrical energy makes many things possible. But have you ever wondered how these devices work?

Every electronic device operates through a combination of electrical components connected in a circuit. A simple circuit consists of a power source, connecting wires, and components such as LEDs, switches, and buzzers. When the circuit is complete, electricity flows through it, allowing the components to function.

About the Course

At Gen Alpha Innovators, we introduce children to the exciting world of electronics and circuit design through hands-on learning using Tinkercad.

Tinkercad is an easy-to-use, web-based design and simulation platform that allows students to explore 3D modeling, build electronic circuits, and experiment with different components in a virtual environment.

This beginner-friendly course introduces young learners to the exciting world of 3D Design, Visual Coding, and Electronics through interactive, hands-on activities. Students learn to design creative 3D models, explore programming concepts using Codeblocks, and build and simulate electronic circuits using virtual components. Through fun, practical projects, they develop creativity, logical thinking, problem-solving, and design skills.

No prior experience is required. Just curiosity, creativity, and a passion for learning!

Training Modes: Online and Offline

Students need:

  • An interest in exploring, experimenting, and creating!

Learning Modules

A progressive, hands-on journey from first designs to practical electronics projects.

Module 1: 3D Design

Learn to create three-dimensional models using basic shapes such as cubes, spheres, cylinders, and cones. Students can design robots, cars, houses, keychains, and other creative objects.

Module 2: Codeblocks

Explore visual programming using colourful coding blocks to create exciting 3D models. Students learn programming concepts such as sequencing, loops, and repetition while developing creativity, logical thinking, and problem-solving skills through projects like Rocket Design and Clock Design.

Module 3: Simple Electronics Circuits Simulation

Discover the fundamentals of electronics by designing and simulating simple electrical circuits using Tinkercad. Students learn how electricity flows through circuits and explore essential components such as LEDs, resistors, batteries, switches, diodes, and sensors. Through hands-on activities like LED Brightness Control, Series and Parallel Circuits, and Touch-Sensitive LED Circuits, students develop a strong foundation in electronics, logical thinking, and practical problem-solving skills.

Module 4: Application-Oriented Electronic Circuits Simulation

Explore the exciting world of real-world electronics by designing and simulating practical projects using Arduino, sensors, LEDs, buzzers, and Micro:bit. Students learn to build innovative solutions such as Password Door Locks, Smart Dustbins, Traffic Light Systems, and Motion Detection Alarms while understanding how electronic components work together. Through hands-on project development, they strengthen their technical skills, logical thinking, creativity, and problem-solving abilities.

Module 5: Certification Test

Evaluate students' understanding through a final assessment covering 3D Design, Codeblocks, basic electronics, circuit simulation, and application-oriented projects.
