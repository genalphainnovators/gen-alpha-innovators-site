Explore the World of Electronic Circuits

Have you ever wondered how lights glow, buzzers make sounds, alarms detect movement and electronic devices work? From automatic lights to security alarms, electronics plays an important role in our everyday lives.

Every electronic device works through a combination of electronic components connected together to form a circuit. Components such as batteries, LEDs, resistors, switches, transistors and sensors work together to perform different functions. When connected correctly, these components allow electricity to flow and make electronic devices work.

About the Course

At Gen Alpha Innovators, we introduce children to the fascinating world of electronics and circuit design through hands-on learning, practical experiments and circuit simulation.

Simple Electronics is a beginner-friendly course that helps students understand the fundamentals of electricity, electronic components and circuit connections. Students explore how different components work, learn to build basic electronic circuits and discover how electronics is used in everyday applications.

Students learn to connect components, understand circuit behaviour, identify errors and build working electronic projects. They develop logical thinking, observation, creativity and problem-solving skills.

No prior electronics experience is required. Just curiosity, creativity and a passion for experimenting!

Training Modes: Online and Offline

Learning Modules

From electronic components to working projects.

Module 1: Introduction to Electronics and Electronic Components

Discover the fundamentals of electronics and explore the components used in everyday electronic devices. Students learn about batteries, LEDs, resistors, switches, buzzers, diodes, transistors and different types of sensors.

They understand the functions of electronic components, their symbols, terminals and applications while developing familiarity with basic electronic equipment.

Module 2: Electronic Circuit Building

Explore the exciting world of circuit building through simple hands-on experiments. Students learn to connect LEDs, resistors, switches, batteries and other basic components to create working electronic circuits.

Discover how transistors and sensors help electronic circuits respond to different conditions. Students learn about components such as LDRs, IR sensors, PIR sensors, rain sensors and other commonly used sensors.

They explore how sensor inputs can control LEDs, buzzers and other output devices. Through practical experiments, students understand automatic switching, light detection, motion detection and basic electronic control systems.

Module 3: Application-Oriented Electronic Projects

Explore how basic electronic concepts are used to develop useful real-world applications. Students combine electronic components, sensors, buzzers, LEDs and other output devices to create practical electronic systems. Students learn how electronic circuits can solve everyday problems.

They apply their knowledge to develop small independent electronic projects, encouraging creativity, confidence and practical problem-solving.

Module 4: Certification Test

Evaluate students’ understanding through a final assessment covering electronic components, electrical circuit fundamentals, basic circuit building and application-oriented electronic projects.
