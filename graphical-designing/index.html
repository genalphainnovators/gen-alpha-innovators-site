Explore the World of Creativity and Digital Design

Graphic design plays an important role in the digital world. From posters and logos to presentations, social media graphics, certificates, and advertisements, visual design helps us communicate ideas creatively and effectively. By learning design principles and using digital tools, students can transform their ideas into creative designs.

About the Course

At Gen Alpha Innovators, we introduce children to the exciting world of graphic design through hands-on creative learning with user-friendly digital design platforms such as Canva.

This beginner-friendly course introduces young learners to the fundamentals of graphic design, including colour combinations, typography, shapes, layouts, image editing, and visual communication. Students learn to design posters, logos, greeting cards, presentations, certificates, social media graphics, and other creative digital projects.

Through practical design activities, students develop creativity, imagination, visual communication, design thinking, and digital skills while learning to express their ideas through attractive and meaningful designs.

No prior experience is required. Just creativity, imagination, and a passion for designing!

Training Modes: Online and Offline

Requirements to Get Started

Students need:

  • A laptop, desktop computer, or compatible tablet/iPad.
  • A stable internet connection.
  • An interest in exploring, designing, and creating!

Learning Modules

A creative journey from design fundamentals to digital projects.

Module 1: Canva Tools and Design Elements

Explore the Canva design platform and learn to use its essential tools, features, and design elements. Students work with text, shapes, icons, images, backgrounds, frames, and layers while creating and editing designs. Through hands-on activities, they build confidence using digital design tools and arranging visual elements creatively, while learning how these elements work together to create attractive designs.

Module 2: Creative Posters, Logos, Flyers, and Flashcards

Learn to create meaningful designs for different purposes using graphic design techniques. Students design posters, logos, greeting cards, invitations, bookmarks, worksheets, and certificates while exploring typography, colours, image placement, and layouts. Through practical projects, they develop creativity, visual storytelling, and design skills.

Module 3: Advanced Graphic Design and Digital Projects

Explore advanced design techniques by creating presentations, social media graphics, thumbnails, book covers, advertisements, and digital branding materials. Students combine images, text, graphics, and layouts to create visually appealing designs for real-world applications. Through project-based learning, they strengthen their creativity, digital communication, problem-solving, and design thinking skills.

Module 4: Certification Test

Evaluate students' understanding through a final assessment covering graphic design fundamentals, Canva tools, colour combinations, typography, layouts, poster design, logo creation, digital cards, and advanced graphic design projects.
